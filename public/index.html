Spanish verb conjugations, one sentence at a time

Every card is a real sentence with the verb blanked out. You see the verb, the person and the tense, then type or recall the conjugated form.

Reading a card

The tense is marked with a symbol on both sides of the gap, so you can tell it at a glance during review.

    Colour-coded regularityThe back of each card says whether the form is regular, morphologically irregular or orthographically irregular, next to the form a regular verb would have.
    Tagged for filteringNotes carry verb::, form:: and person:: tags, so you can build a filtered deck in Anki for just the subjunctive, or just ser and ir.
    ImportingIn Anki, choose File → Import and pick the downloaded file. Re-importing a newer version updates your existing cards and keeps your progress.
